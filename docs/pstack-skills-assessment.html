The main mismatch. pstack treats a running application and its PR as the normal unit of work. Here, the unit may be a theorem change, a simulation result, a generated dataset, an oscilloscope trace, firmware timing, or a manuscript claim. Any adopted verification skill must name the evidence for each kind of artifact.

Recommended adoption plan

Do not copy 22 skill folders into your setup. Package the useful rules around the activities you perform. This keeps triggers clear and avoids a pile of overlapping instructions.

Build one research verification skill first

Combine prove-it-works, sequence-verifiable-units, blast-radius, tdd, and make-operations-idempotent. Give it four evidence modes: MATLAB tests and plots, LaTeX build and claim checks, ESP32 build plus lab trace, and generated-data equivalence. This is the highest return because the repo already links all four.

Add an explanation and archaeology skill

Adapt how, why, and teach. Replace corporate Slack, Sentry, and analytics searches with git history, ADRs, working notes, reviewer feedback, literature, manuscript citations, and experiment logs. Keep the distinction between mechanics and motivation.

Strengthen the existing design skills

Keep domain-modeling and grill-me. Fold in foundational-thinking, boundary-discipline, type-system-discipline, separate-before-serializing-shared-state, and the caller-first sketch from architect. Do not import its mandatory four-model arena.

Create a research writing companion

Keep your exact unslop copy. Add the useful parts of technical-writing, then supply what pstack lacks: claim-to-source traceability, notation consistency, cautious causal language, figure and table references, and separation of derivation, result, and interpretation.

Split live verification by bounded context

Use create-verification-skill as a pattern, not a single generated skill. Create separate controls for simulations/matlab-sim, embedded/esp32_idf, and the thesis build. Their launch commands, equipment, evidence, and cleanup rules are too different to share one driver.

Try decision logs only on long experiments

Adopt show-me-your-work for multi-day tuning, hardware timing characterization, migrations, and paper revisions with many rejected alternatives. Keep normal fixes in the existing TODO history. Logging every small change would create paperwork, not confidence.

Comparison with your current skills

Ten skill files were found on disk. Five are registered in this Pi session. The others still matter as evidence of your preferred workflow.

domain-modeling

Maintains glossaries, context maps, and selective ADRs while challenging fuzzy terms.

Keep it. It is better suited to this repository than pstack's principle-model-the-domain. Borrow pstack's data-structure advice for code, not its name.

grill-me and grill-with-docs

Resolve design decisions one question at a time with stable identifiers and recorded decisions.

Keep them. interrogate reviews an artifact after it exists. It complements grilling instead of replacing it.

prototype

Builds a throwaway logic or UI probe, exposes state, and records the answer.

Keep it. Merge pstack's rule to compare two or three structurally different options when the design has no precedent.

unslop

Removes AI writing tells and requires plain, specific prose.

Do not install pstack's copy. The bodies are identical. Your file only adds disable-model-invocation: false.

handoff

Writes a compact continuation document and points at existing artifacts instead of duplicating them.

Keep it. Borrow recall's live-state check and short thread statuses. Cursor transcript paths and corporate evidence sources do not transfer directly.

todo

Keeps a persistent project TODO and history current during work.

Keep it as the task record. pstack's repeated in-memory todolist requirements would duplicate this and add ceremony.

html-doc and html-concept

Create standalone visual study documents and focused concept explainers.

No pstack skill replaces them. teach can route to these when diagrams are the clearest teaching form.

mcp-scripting

Discovers and coordinates MCP tools through the Pi adapter.

Keep it. It is the portability layer needed to rewrite pstack's why, recall, and model-dependent workflows for Pi.
