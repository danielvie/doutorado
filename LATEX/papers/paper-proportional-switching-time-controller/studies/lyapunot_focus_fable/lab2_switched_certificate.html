Things to try.
1. Manual: hold space exactly while the state crosses the flat directions and release elsewhere. You can make |x|² (gray) climb repeatedly. You are pumping the swing.
2. Now watch the green curve while you do your worst. It never rises. That is what the SDP certificate promised, and this is what a proof for all switching signals feels like in the hand.
3. Adversarial mode implements the worst pumping law (grow the radius as fast as possible at every instant). Even that only slows the green slope down; per revolution the radius still shrinks to about 0.76 of itself, matching Part E of the MATLAB script.
4. Periodic: sweep the half-period slider and find the rhythm that hurts most. Notice it is the resonant-looking one, not the fastest one.
5. Show the circle level sets while pumping: the trajectory visibly exits circles. Switch to the degree-8 sets: it never exits one. The blade shape from the tutorial's Figure 6 is exactly what "closed under every switch" requires.
