\section*{Daniel versus MPC}

The threefold initial-error trial uses the same converter, LQR weights,
$286\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ period, and $3\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ dwell bound.
Blue denotes \texttt{daniel} and dashed orange denotes \texttt{mpc-5};
the MPC suffix is its prediction horizon in cycles.
The panels show cycle-start error, minimum applied dwell, and first-cycle dwells.

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth,height=.78\textheight,keepaspectratio]{\evidence/comparison.pdf}
\end{center}

\clearpage
Exact state trajectories over the first twelve nominal periods compare
Daniel and five-cycle MPC with the dashed reference. MPC-5 settles earlier
than Daniel but produces negative capacitor voltage. This trial also starts
with negative current; it tests the mathematical model, not validated
hardware operation. No nonlinear stability guarantee follows from these plots.
