## Main-text figures

Five main-text figures use six PDFs and one TikZ source under `latex/figures/`.

1. `timing_coordinates.pdf`, `dwell_coupling.pdf`, and `inadmissible_request.pdf` form one explanatory figure near the problem formulation. They show the fixed endpoints and movable interior instants, the coupled effect of one illustrative offset, and the first-cycle raw boundary-order inversion. Generated by `paper.export_timing_diagrams` from current benchmark/response data, with PNG previews under `results/figures/`.
2. `common_p_geometry.tex`, a two-dimensional schematic of the common-P argument: one unit level ellipse, both endpoint images, and their interpolating segment. Included directly by `latex/main.tex` and rendered by TikZ during `task build`. This is explanatory geometry, not converter data or a nonlinear region of attraction.
3. `trajectory_comparison.pdf`, two panels: dense continuous-time physical-state paths for conditioned feedback and open loop over the same 100 cycles, and normalized cycle-start error over the full open-loop horizon on logarithmic axes. Shading connects the first 100 cycles to the 3D panel; dots mark actual feedback-first and open-loop-persistent samples below the selected 0.01 reporting threshold. The caption identifies the panels and markers; the text explains the displayed ranges, defines the dimensionless error, and distinguishes the threshold from stability and hardware requirements. Rendered by `paper.plot_trajectories` from the data produced by `paper.export_trajectories`.
4. `conditioned_control_response.pdf`, first 25 cycles of closed-loop/open-loop error, the applied fraction beta, and requested/applied minimum dwell on aligned axes. Active scaling and forbidden dwell values are shaded. Rendered by `paper.plot_conditioned_response`, called by `paper.export_figures` or `paper.replot_figures`.
5. `state_time_comparison.pdf`, three aligned continuous-time voltage/current plots over the first 12 cycles. Closed loop, open loop, and the repeated reference periodic waveform share the time axis; their original within-cycle sample times remain distinct. Rendered by `paper.plot_trajectories` using `paper.state_history_data`.

The 3D paths and state-time curves use exact within-interval samples, not only polygons connecting switching-boundary states. The reference waveform is neither the cycle average nor a constant anchor. The long-horizon error panel uses phase-aligned cycle starts and omits cycle zero on its logarithmic axis.

`task replot` redraws the numerical figures from saved data. They export at the final 160 mm manuscript width with 9–10 pt labels/legends and 8–9 pt numerical ticks/labels, without tight-crop rescaling. The common-P schematic remains compiled by TikZ.

## Tables and displayed certificate

- `tab:parameters` contains converter, schedule, normalization, and LQR parameters.
- `tab:results` contains spectral radii, conditioning statistics, dwell durations, and error-threshold cycles.
- `eq:numerical-p` and `eq:numerical-margins` display the common Lyapunov matrix and directly checked eigenvalue margins.

The manuscript uses generated values from `latex/metrics.tex` and `latex/lyapunov_metrics.tex`.

## Supporting figures

`latex/figures/open_loop_convergence.pdf` is retained as the earlier detailed long-run state/3D view. It is no longer included or regenerated by the article plotter; the complete long-horizon numerical records remain unchanged.

`latex/figures/reference_schedule_orbit.pdf` remains generated by `paper.export_figures` as a supporting view of the reconciled schedule, periodic states, and cycle averages, but is not included in the manuscript. The main text uses the timing figure for the schedule and the trajectory comparisons for the reference periodic trajectory and anchor; cycle averages remain stated in the text.

`latex/figures/invariant_raw_action_region.pdf` remains generated evidence for the supporting region analysis but is not included in the manuscript. `results/figures/first_cycle_conditioning.pdf` is an auxiliary detailed dwell view; `results/figures/conditioned_control_response.pdf` is the source copy of the promoted main-text response figure.

The older standalone comparisons remain under `studies/trajectory-comparison/results/`. Older experiments are preserved under `archive/legacy-experiments/figures/`.
