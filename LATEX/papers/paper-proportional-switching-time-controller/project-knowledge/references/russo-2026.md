## Bibliographic identity

A. Russo, G. P. Incremona, and P. Colaneri, “Stabilization of Switched Affine Systems With Dwell-Time Constraint,” *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control*, 71(5), 3030–3043, 2026. DOI: `10.1109/TAC.2025.3634172`.

## Problem

The paper studies stabilization of switched-affine systems while enforcing a minimum dwell-time constraint.

## System and setting

The controller uses state-dependent switching laws. The active mode can change according to the state, subject to dwell-time behavior.

## Approach

The paper uses differential Lyapunov equations and Lyapunov–Metzler inequalities, together with quadratic-cost bounds, to analyze and synthesize the switching law.

## Main contribution

The source develops a dwell-constrained state-dependent switching stabilization framework for switched-affine systems.

## Evidence

The paper reports three numerical examples.

## Assumptions and limitations

The switching actuator selects modes through a state-dependent law. This is different from preserving a prescribed mode order and adjusting its interior switching instants once per cycle.
