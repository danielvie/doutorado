## Bibliographic identity

S. Boyd, L. El Ghaoui, E. Feron, and V. Balakrishnan, *Linear Matrix Inequalities in System and Control Theory*, Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, 1994.

## Problem

The book develops linear-matrix-inequality methods for analysis and synthesis in systems and control.

## System and setting

It covers general continuous- and discrete-time control problems expressed through convex matrix inequalities, including Lyapunov and stability conditions.

## Approach

The book formulates control and stability conditions as LMIs and explains their convex optimization structure, numerical solution, and interpretation.

## Main contribution

The source is a foundational reference for LMI-based control analysis and synthesis.

## Evidence

The book provides theory, derivations, and worked control examples rather than a project-specific experiment.

## Assumptions and limitations

It supplies general mathematical machinery. It does not analyze the paper's switching-time actuator or dwell conditioner.

## Concepts useful for later reading

- Lyapunov inequalities;
- common quadratic Lyapunov functions;
- semidefinite programming;
- convex matrix inequalities;
- induced-norm stability tests.
