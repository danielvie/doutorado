# Flieller, Riedinger, and Louis (2006)

## Bibliographic identity

D. Flieller, P. Riedinger, and J.-P. Louis, “Computation and Stability of Limit Cycles in Hybrid Systems,” *Nonlinear Analysis: Theory, Methods & Applications*, 64(2), 352–367, 2006.

## Problem

The paper studies computation of periodic solutions and local stability for hybrid systems with switching events.

## System and setting

The setting is a hybrid system with event-dependent trajectories and a periodic orbit. Switching times and event transitions affect the sampled return behavior.

## Approach

The paper uses switching-time sensitivities in the computation of hybrid limit cycles. It relates the Jacobian of the sampled return map to local stability of the cycle.

## Main contribution

The source provides an earlier treatment of switching-time sensitivity for hybrid limit-cycle computation and sampled-time local-stability tests.

## Evidence

The paper develops analytical results and computational procedures for hybrid periodic solutions and their stability.

## Assumptions and limitations

The source addresses hybrid limit-cycle computation and return-map stability. Its setting is not the same as a prescribed fixed-period mode sequence with a static timing-offset feedback law and dwell conditioning.

## Concepts useful for later reading

- hybrid limit cycles;
- return or Poincaré maps;
- switching-time sensitivity;
- sampled-time local stability.
