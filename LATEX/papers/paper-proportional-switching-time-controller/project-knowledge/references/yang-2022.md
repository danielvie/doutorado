Read when: studying fixed-frequency direct MPC and fast constrained solvers

## Bibliographic identity

Q. Yang, P. Karamanakos, W. Tian, X. Gao, X. Li, T. Geyer, and R. Kennel, “Computationally Efficient Fixed Switching Frequency Direct Model Predictive Control,” *IEEE Transactions on Power Electronics*, 37(3), 2761–2777, 2022.

## Problem

The paper addresses the execution cost of direct model predictive control when the switching frequency must remain fixed and decisions must fit a short control interval.

## System and setting

The work concerns fixed-switching-frequency power-electronics control, including converter and drive applications.

## Approach

The paper formulates switching-instant decisions through a constrained quadratic program and uses a custom solver with early termination to reduce execution time.

## Main contribution

The source presents a computationally efficient fixed-frequency direct MPC method that handles timing decisions within a tight implementation budget.

## Evidence

The paper reports experiments on a 3 kW two-level inverter and induction-machine drive.

## Assumptions and limitations

The method is an online optimization approach with implementation-specific timing. It does not use the fixed mode-order, radial scalar conditioning mechanism described by this project.
