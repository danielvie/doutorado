## Bibliographic identity

C. Briat and A. Seuret, “Affine Characterizations of Minimal and Mode-Dependent Dwell-Times for Uncertain Linear Switched Systems,” *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control*, 58(5), 1304–1310, 2013.

## Problem

The paper studies how minimum and mode-dependent dwell-time requirements affect stability of uncertain linear switched systems.

## System and setting

The setting is a switched linear system with uncertainty and restrictions on the time spent in modes. The dwell conditions can vary with the active mode.

## Approach

The paper derives affine characterizations and computable stability conditions for minimal and mode-dependent dwell times. The results use Lyapunov and matrix-inequality techniques.

## Main contribution

The source supplies general dwell-time stability and feasibility machinery for uncertain linear switched systems.

## Evidence

The paper provides theoretical conditions and numerical demonstrations of the dwell-time characterizations.

## Assumptions and limitations

The source concerns switched-system stability under dwell restrictions. It does not define the project’s radial contraction of a raw switching-instant action or its endpoint common-quadratic analysis.
