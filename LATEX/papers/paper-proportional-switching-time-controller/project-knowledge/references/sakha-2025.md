## Bibliographic identity

M. S. Sakha and R. Kamalapurkar, “Switched Optimal Control with Dwell Time Constraints,” arXiv preprint, 2025. DOI: `10.48550/arXiv.2501.05548`.

## Problem

The paper studies switched optimal-control problems in which switching times must obey dwell-time constraints.

## System and setting

The setting is a switched optimal-control problem with switching signals and timing decisions subject to minimum dwell requirements.

## Approach

The paper combines continuous optimization with a filter that removes or rejects switching times that violate dwell-time constraints. It also addresses the promotion of feasible bang-bang switching behavior.

## Main contribution

The source develops an optimization-based method for switched optimal control with dwell-time enforcement.

## Evidence

The paper reports numerical examples.

## Assumptions and limitations

The method optimizes switching behavior and uses a filtering mechanism. It does not use a fixed-period static timing-feedback law with a closed-form maximal radial scaling factor.
